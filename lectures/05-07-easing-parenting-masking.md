**Concepts to demo:**
- Easing and interpolation deeper dive:
	- linear, easy ease, ease out only, ease in only
- Parenting
	- Pickwhip tagline to title
- Masks (shape and via mask path)
	- Mask in AE are a property of one layer, drawn directly on it
- Track Mattes
	- Track matte = relationship between two layers, where the top one controls the visibility of the one below. Use a track matte when you want to use one layer's shape, including its animation, to control another
	- More flexible but more complicated
	- https://www.are.na/screwjack/type-in-motion-58xjn2ijhieicated




**Exercise + Homework**
- Animate your poster
- Bring your poster into AE and animate it appearing on screen
	- 5-8 seconds. 
	- At least four animated elements
	- Use easing on every keyframe 
	- Use at least one mask reveal


	- Considerations:
		- What makes one element more important than another? how does the animation support that?  
		- It's the poster _arriving_, not a movie. Title sequence energy.
		- Pick a vibe — urgent, calm, mysterious, playful — and let timing decisions follow from it.
		- Don't animate every property on every layer. Pick what serves the idea.
