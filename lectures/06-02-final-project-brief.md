# Final Project Brief

*June 2, 2026* · [← All lectures](index.html)

Your personal site already contains the work from this semester. For the final project, make at least one new piece _specifically for the site_ — something that takes advantage of being on the web. It can be small or ambitious. It should reflect something you actually want to make.

**Examples of what counts:**

- A new animation made in AE, embedded with context
- An interactive piece using HTML/CSS/JS — a small game, a generative pattern, something that responds to clicks or movement
- A photo essay or written piece with intentional layout
- A "hidden" page only reachable through clues on the main page
- A daily-something page (one image per day for two weeks, etc.)
- A page that does something weird with type, color, or layout that wouldn't work in any other medium
